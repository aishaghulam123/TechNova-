Frequently asked

The stuff people
actually ask us.

Still curious? Reach out — a real human replies within a day.

Do I need a technical background?

No. Our foundations sprint gets absolute beginners production‑ready before the main cohort starts. Roughly a third of every cohort is career‑changing.

How does the job guarantee work?

Graduate the program, complete the hiring sprint and apply weekly. If you aren't placed within six months, we refund your tuition. Full terms in the enrollment agreement.

Are the sessions live or recorded?

Both. Every workshop is live and interactive, plus recorded so you can review on your schedule or catch up if life happens.

Can I do this while working full time?

Yes. Our part‑time tracks require 12–15 hours a week. Full‑time tracks are 40+ and finish faster.

What if I fall behind?

Mentors run weekly 1‑on‑1s. If you slip, we adjust your plan or defer you to the next cohort at no cost.
